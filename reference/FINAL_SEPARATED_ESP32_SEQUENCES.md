##### ***ESP-NOW Coconut Wine Sequence***



**A) POURING THE SAP INTO INITIAL CONTAINER**

MANUAL OPENING OF VALVE TO CONTAINER USING LCD OR MOBILE DEVICE (WIRELESS)

V1 \[ESP32-1]

DETECTION OF VARIABLE IF IT IS THE RIGHT VARIABLE OR NOT USING THE RIGHT TRESHOLD

PH SENSOR, ALCOHOL SENSOR, ULTRA SONIC (Inputs routed to \[ESP32-1])



**B) TRANSFERING TO PROCESS CONTAINER 1**

AFTER INSPECTION OF VARIABLE THE VALVES AND PUMP SYNCRONOUSLY OPENING

V2 \[ESP32-1], PUMP\_1 \[ESP32-1], AND V3 \[ESP32-1] WILL OPEN

AFTER TRANSFER ALL VALVES AND PUMP1 WILL CLOSE



**C) CLEANING AND DRAINING THE INITIAL CONTAINER**

Opening PUMPS AND VALVES

THE HP\_PUMP \[ESP32-1], CV1 \[ESP32-1], DV1 \[ESP32-1], AND PUMP\_5 \[ESP32-1] WILL OPEN

AFTER CLEANING AND DRAINING ALL WILL CLOSE.



**D) CLEANING HOSE PATHS AND BACK FLUSHING FOR CLEANING F1 AND FF1**

Opening PUMP AND VALVES

THE CV1 \[ESP32-1], DV1 \[ESP32-1], AND V2 \[ESP32-1] WILL CLOSE.

THEN THE HP\_PUMP \[ESP32-1], CV2 \[ESP32-1], PUMP\_1 \[ESP32-1], FV2 \[ESP32-1], AND PUMP\_5 \[ESP32-1] WILL OPEN.

AFTER THAT THE BACKFLUSH CLEANING WILL OCCUR, THE CV3 \[ESP32-1] AND FV1 \[ESP32-1] WILL OPEN AND THE PUMP 1 \[ESP32-1] WILL CLOSE

AFTER BACKFLUSH ALL VALVES AND PUMPS WILL CLOSE



**E) PROCESSING CONTAINER 1**

WILL MEASURES THE VALUE OF ETHANOL AND PH CONTENT OF THE WINE

PH SENSOR AND ALCOHOL SENSOR (Inputs routed to \[ESP32-2])



**F) START SWITCHING CONTAINER AND TRANSFERING THE WINE TO REMOVE LATAK FROM PROCESS CONTAINER 1 TO PROCESS CONTAINER 2**

Opening PUMP and Valves

V4 \[ESP32-1], PUMP\_2 \[ESP32-1], V5 \[ESP32-1] WILL OPEN

AFTER TRANSFER ALL PUMP AND VALVE WILL CLOSE.



**G) CLEANING AND DRAINING PROCESS CONTAINER 1**

Opening PUMPS AND VALVES (ESP-NOW REQUIRED HERE: ESP2 sends signal to ESP1 for shared pumps)

THE HP\_PUMP \[ESP32-1], CV4 \[ESP32-2], DV2 \[ESP32-2], AND PUMP\_5 \[ESP32-1] WILL OPEN.

AFTER CLEANING AND DRAINING ALL WILL CLOSE.



**H) CLEANING HOSE PATHS AND BACK FLUSHING FOR CLEANING F2 AND FF2**

Opening PUMP AND VALVES

THE CV4 \[ESP32-2], DV2 \[ESP32-2], AND V4 \[ESP32-1] WILL CLOSE.

THEN THE HP\_PUMP \[ESP32-1], CV5 \[ESP32-2], PUMP\_2 \[ESP32-1], FV4 \[ESP32-2], AND PUMP\_5 \[ESP32-1] WILL OPEN.

AFTER THAT THE BACKFLUSH CLEANING WILL OCCUR, THE CV6 \[ESP32-2], AND FV3 \[ESP32-2] WILL OPEN AND THE PUMP\_2 \[ESP32-1] WILL CLOSE

AFTER BACKFLUSH ALL VALVES AND PUMPS WILL CLOSE



**I) PROCESSING CONTAINER 2**

WILL MEASURES THE VALUE OF ETHANOL AND PH CONTENT OF THE WINE

PH SENSOR AND ALCOHOL SENSOR (Inputs routed to \[ESP32-2])



**J) START SWITCHING CONTAINER AND TRANSFERING THE WINE TO REMOVE LATAK FROM PROCESS CONTAINER 2 TO PROCESS CONTAINER 1**

Opening PUMP and Valves

V6 \[ESP32-2], PUMP 3 \[ESP32-2], V7 \[ESP32-2]

AFTER TRANSFER ALL WILL CLOSE.



**K) CLEANING AND DRAINING PROCESS CONTAINER 2**

Opening PUMPS AND VALVES (ESP-NOW REQUIRED)

THE HP\_PUMP \[ESP32-1], CV7 \[ESP32-2], DV3 \[ESP32-2], AND PUMP\_5 \[ESP32-1] WILL OPEN.

AFTER CLEANING AND DRAINING ALL WILL CLOSE.



**L)  CLEANING HOSE PATHS AND BACK FLUSHING FOR CLEANING F3 AND FF3**

Opening PUMP AND VALVES

THE CV7 \[ESP32-2], DV3 \[ESP32-2], AND V6 \[ESP32-2] WILL CLOSE.

THEN THE HP\_PUMP \[ESP32-1], CV8 \[ESP32-2], PUMP\_3 \[ESP32-2], FV5 \[ESP32-2], AND PUMP\_5 \[ESP32-1] WILL OPEN.

AFTER THAT THE BACKFLUSH CLEANING WILL OCCUR, THE CV9 \[ESP32-2], AND FV6 \[ESP32-2] WILL OPEN AND THE PUMP\_3 \[ESP32-2] WILL CLOSE

AFTER BACKFLUSH ALL VALVES AND PUMPS WILL CLOSE



*(REPEAT DAILY FOR ONE WEEK EITHER E->H or I->L depending on where the Tuba is)*

*(AFTER ONE WEEK)*



**M) TRANSFERING FROM PROCESS CONTAINER 2 OR CONTAINER 1 TO FINAL OUTPUT CONTAINER**

Opening PUMP AND VALVES

V8 \[ESP32-3] OR V9 \[ESP32-3] WILL OPEN DEPENDS ON WHAT IS THE LAST CONTAINER HOLDS THE WINE, PUMP\_4 \[ESP32-3] GOING TO FINAL CONTAINER

AFTER TRANSFER ALL VALVES AND PUMP WILL CLOSE.



**N)  CLEANING HOSE PATHS**

Opening PUMP AND VALVES (Heavy ESP-NOW coordination required across all 3 boards)

THE HP\_PUMP \[ESP32-1], CV8 \[ESP32-2], DV4 \[ESP32-3], PUMP\_4 \[ESP32-3] AND PUMP\_5 \[ESP32-1] WILL OPEN

AFTER CLEANING ALL THE VALVES AND PUMPS WILL CLOSE



**O) AFTER TRANSFER RE DO J->K STEPS FOR CLEANING, BACK FLUSHING, AND DRAINING OF CONTAINERS.**

(Follow mapped pins for ESP32-2 and ESP32-1)

AFTER USER GETS THE OUTPUT PRODUCT:

THE PRODUCT CAN GET USING MANUAL BUTTON CAME FROM LCD OR MOBILE DEVICE(WIRELESS)

Trigger V10 \[ESP32-3]



**P) CLEANING AND DRAINING OUTPUT CONTAINER**

Opening PUMPS AND VALVES (ESP-NOW REQUIRED)

THE HP\_PUMP \[ESP32-1], CV10 \[ESP32-3], DV5 \[ESP32-3], AND PUMP\_5 \[ESP32-1] WILL OPEN.

AFTER CLEANING AND DRAINING ALL WILL CLOSE.

##### ***ESP-NOW Vinegar Sequence***

**A) POURING THE SAP INTO INITIAL CONTAINER**

MANUAL OPENING OF VALVE TO CONTAINER USING LCD OR MOBILE DEVICE (WIRELESS)

VV1 \[ESP32-3]

DETECTION OF VARIABLE IF IT IS THE RIGHT VARIABLE OR NOT USING THE RIGHT TRESHOLD

PH SENSOR, ALCOHOL SENSOR, ULTRASONIC SENSOR (Inputs routed to \[ESP32-3])



**B) MOTHER VINEGAR ADDING TO INITIAL CONTAINER BASE ON ULTRASONIC SENSOR**

DCP1 (MOTHERV\_VALVE/PUMP) \[ESP32-3]



**C) TRANSFER INITIAL CONTAINER TO PROCESS CONTAINER 1, or PROCESS CONTAINER 2 OF VINEGAR depending on which is available**

Opening PUMP AND VALVES

VV2 \[ESP32-3],  VPUMP\_1 \[ESP32-3], VV3 \[ESP32-3] (FOR CONTAINER 1)

VV2 \[ESP32-3],  VPUMP\_1 \[ESP32-3], VV4 \[ESP32-3] (FOR CONTAINER 2)

AFTER TRANSFERING ALL VALVES AND PUMP WILL BE CLOSE



**D.1) CLEANING AND DRAINING THE INITIAL CONTAINER**

Opening PUMPS AND VALVES (ESP-NOW Required to trigger ESP32-1)

THE HP\_PUMP \[ESP32-1], CVV1 \[ESP32-3], DVV1 \[ESP32-3], AND VPUMP\_4 \[ESP32-4] WILL OPEN

AFTER CLEANING ALL THE VALVE AND PUMPS WILL CLOSE



**D.2) CLEANING HOSE PATHS**

\- OPENING OF PUMPS AND VALVES (ESP-NOW Required)

\* THE HP\_PUMP \[ESP32-1], CVV2 \[ESP32-4] AND DVV2 \[ESP32-4] WILL OPEN

\- AFTER CLEANING ALL THE VALVES AND PUMPS WILL CLOSE



**E) PROCESSING CONTAINER 1 or 2**

Opening PUMPS for Aeration

THE AP \[ESP32-4] WILL OPEN AND AV1 \[ESP32-4] OR AV2 \[ESP32-4] WILL OPEN DEPENDS ON AVAILABILITY OF CONTAINER

Monitoring Fermentation Stabilization and Threshold

Temperature Sensor and Probe, PH Sensor, Alcohol Sensor (Inputs routed to \[ESP32-4])



**F) TRANSFER TO FINAL output from PROCESSING CONTAINER 1 or 2**

Opening PUMPS AND VALVES (ESP-NOW Required for Container 1 mapping)

VPUMP\_2 \[ESP32-4], VV5 \[ESP32-4], and VV7 \[ESP32-5] for CONTAINER 1

VPUMP\_3 \[ESP32-4], VV6 \[ESP32-4], and VV8 \[ESP32-5] for CONTAINER 2

AFTER TRANSFERING ALL PUMP AND VALVES WILL CLOSE



**G) CLEANING AND DRAINING THE CONTAINER 1 or 2**

Opening PUMPS AND VALVES (ESP-NOW Required for Shared Pumps)

HP\_PUMP \[ESP32-1], CVV3 \[ESP32-4], DVV3 \[ESP32-4] for CONTAINER 1

HP\_PUMP \[ESP32-1], CVV5 \[ESP32-4], DVV4 \[ESP32-4] FOR CONTAINER 2

EITHER OF THE 2 CONTAINER, THE VPUMP\_4 \[ESP32-4] WILL OPEN

AFTER CLEANING ALL THE VALVE AND PUMPS WILL CLOSE



**H) CLEANING HOSE PATHS**

OPENING OF PUMPS AND VALVES (Heavy ESP-NOW coordination required)

THE HP\_PUMP \[ESP32-1], CVV4 \[ESP32-4], AND VPUMP\_2 \[ESP32-4] WILL OPEN FOR CONTAINER 1 PATH. DVV5 \[ESP32-5] WILL STILL OPEN.

THE HP\_PUMP \[ESP32-1], CVV6 \[ESP32-5], AND VPUMP\_3 \[ESP32-4] WILL OPEN FOR CONTAINER 2. DVV5 \[ESP32-5] WILL STILL OPEN.

AFTER CLEANING ALL THE VALVES AND PUMPS WILL CLOSE

(AFTER GETTING THE FINAL OUTPUT)



**I) CLEANING FINAL OUTPUT CONTAINER**

Opening PUMPS AND VALVES (ESP-NOW Required)

HP\_PUMP \[ESP32-1], CVV7 \[ESP32-5], DVV6 \[ESP32-5], VPUMP\_4 \[ESP32-4]

AFTER CLEANING ALL THE VALVE AND PUMPS WILL CLOSE
